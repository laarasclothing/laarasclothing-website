Pickup & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Laara's doesn't ship by courier. Our own riders pick up your fabric and deliver your stitched clothes in the cities we serve, currently Bhiwani, Haryana.

1. Charges

  • Pickup + delivery: ₹59 on every order (alterations included)
  • Trial at your door: free on every order (you can choose to skip it)
  • Repeat trial: ₹49, only if you change your mind, your own measurement was off, or you skipped the trial. Free if the fit problem came from our tailor or our measurement visit.
  • Home measurement visit: ₹50

Launch offer: free pickup and delivery plus ₹100 off on the first 500 orders (one per customer).

2. Timing

  • Pickup slots: 8 am – 6 pm in 2-hour slots (8–10, 10–12, 12–2, 2–4, 4–6), usually the same or next day.
  • The ready date is confirmed with your order; it depends on the garment. Express stitching is available for some garments at extra cost.
  • Delivery is made within 1 day of the outfit being ready, at a time agreed on WhatsApp.

3. At your door

The rider notes your fabric length at pickup. At delivery you can check the outfit; the rider waits while you try it (up to 15 minutes), records any changes and takes them back to the tailor. Please make sure someone is available at the agreed time; a repeat visit caused by no one being home may be charged.

4. Service area

We serve addresses within Bhiwani city limits. Message us to check your area.
