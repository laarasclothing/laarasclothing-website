Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how Laara's Clothing by aRu (LAARAS CLOTHING (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED (incorporation in progress)) collects and uses personal data when you use laarasclothing.in or order from us on WhatsApp or by phone. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

1. What we collect

  • Customers: name, phone number, address and location for pickup and delivery, body measurements, photos of designs or fabric you share, order history and payment status.
  • Tailors and riders: name, phone, address, identity and KYC documents, bank or UPI details for payouts, photos of work and, for riders, driving licence and police-verification documents.
  • Website: basic technical data (browser, pages visited) kept by our hosting provider. We don't use advertising trackers on this website.

2. Why we use it

To take and deliver your orders, share the details a tailor or rider needs to do the job, take payments and pay partners, provide support, prevent fraud, and meet legal and tax requirements. We'll only send offers on WhatsApp or SMS if you agree, and you can opt out at any time.

3. Who we share it with

Only with the staff and rider handling your order (just what they need), our payment partner (such as Razorpay), identity-verification providers for partner sign-up, cloud and messaging providers that run our services, and government authorities when the law requires. We never sell your data.

4. How long we keep it

We keep order data as long as your account is active and as long as tax and accounting laws require (usually 8 years for invoices). Measurements and photos are deleted when you delete your profile or ask us to.

5. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or nominate someone to act for you, by writing to admin@laarasclothing.in. We reply within 30 days.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited to people who need it, and card payments are handled entirely by our payment partner.

7. Children

Orders must be placed by an adult (18+). Measurements for children are given by a parent or guardian.
