Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Regular stitching is paid on delivery, so in most cases there is nothing to refund. This policy covers cancellations, fit problems and the few cases where you have paid in advance.

1. Cancelling an order

  • Before pickup: free.
  • After pickup, before cutting: free; we return your fabric and charge only the delivery fee for bringing it back.
  • After the fabric is cut: the order can't be cancelled, because the fabric can't be reused. You pay the stitching charge for work done.
  • Bridal and custom (Studio) orders: the advance is refundable in full if you cancel before the fabric is bought or cut. After that, the cost of material bought and work done is deducted and the rest refunded.

2. Fit and quality problems

  • Fit problems from our stitching or our measurement visit: free alteration.
  • Fit problems from measurements you gave yourself: alteration at our normal alteration rate.
  • Stitching defects: free repair after our quality check.
  • Outfit not made as agreed (wrong design, colour or missing work) and it can't be fixed: full refund of the stitching charge, plus compensation for the fabric if it was spoiled.
  • Fabric lost or damaged in our care: compensation up to the reasonable value of the fabric.

Please report a problem within 48 hours of delivery or trial, with photos, on WhatsApp or by email.

3. Late delivery

If we miss the confirmed ready date, tell us and we'll offer a discount or a free delivery. If we miss an event date you told us about at booking, the stitching charge is refunded.

4. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI, card or bank) within 5–7 working days. Cash payments are refunded by UPI or bank transfer.
